## Project Name:

# GoDrive

## Live URL

Live Website: https://godrive-seven.vercel.app/

## Key Features: 

1. Easy Car Booking – Browse available cars and book your preferred vehicle with a simple booking process.
2. User Authentication – Secure registration and login with email/password and Google authentication using Better Auth.
3. Car Management – Add, view, and manage car listings with details such as rental price, car type, seating capacity, and availability.
4. Real-Time Car Availability – Check car availability before booking to make the rental process easier.
5. Booking Management – View your bookings and manage your rental reservations from your account.
